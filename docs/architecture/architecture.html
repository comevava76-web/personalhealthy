Functional overview

What the app does, who does what and in which order. A blood-pressure diary for Android, with a web dashboard and reports for the doctor.

The flow from the phone to the doctor

The user's path

  1. Installs the app from the public link /download (later from Google Play).
  2. Signs in with Google once, through OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect (a signed ID token, never the password), and gives privacy consent.
  3. Accepts the terms of use, binding, at the first installation and after every update: without acceptance the app does not open. Every acceptance is recorded (anonymous code, phone, versions, text fingerprint, date).
  4. Every day unlocks with the phone's fingerprint or PIN, and stays signed in until “Sign out”.
  5. Records a reading: by voice (SYS, DIA, PUL, checked and confirmed on screen) or by photographing the display (Upgrade).
  6. Looks back: last reading, the week, the Report with charts and tiles.
  7. Shares: PDF or Excel from the phone, or Web Dashboard → “Send to the doctor”.
  8. Changes phone: signs in with Google and finds everything. Signs out or deletes the account at any time.

Tiers

BASE · 3 $ A YEAR through Google Play, cancel any time: voice readings, charts, PDF and Excel reports, reminders, Web Dashboard, doctor links.

UPGRADE scanning with AI: a photo of the monitor's display and, coming, medical documents (values into tables and charts). Uses the user's own Anthropic key: they pay Anthropic directly.

Fixed rules

  • No diagnosis, no advice, no judgement of the values: no coloured zones, no “normal”.
  • Labels always SYS, DIA, PUL. Pressure and pulse never in the same chart.
  • Charts: 7 days, one dot per day = the mean of that day's readings (explained before the charts), value next to it, axis with day numbers only.
  • The same A4 PDF from the app and from the web; the web downloads it at once as a file.
  • No red anywhere: SYS violet, DIA teal, PUL amber. No colour means “good” or “bad”.
  • Scan is on only when Anthropic has just said yes (valid key, credit): a minimal request on opening if the last check is over an hour old, after every error and with “Check again”.
  • Readings kept at most 365 days, deleted nightly; only the acceptances of the terms remain, as proof.
  • The owner pays only the server (free tier) and alone can switch installed apps off remotely; every user pays their own AI.

Database tables

persons accounts (anonymous code, encrypted fingerprint of the Google account, no email; subscription sub_token, sub_until, sub_state) · measurements readings · scans photo readings · ledger credit and costs · person_keys encrypted Anthropic keys · acceptances accepted terms (append-only) · web_codes, web_sessions, web_shares web access and doctor links · settings, invites.

Security and data

  • Phone: a private key that never leaves the phone signs every request; the server refuses bad signatures or clocks off by more than 5 minutes.
  • Web: a 60 s one-time code in the # part of the address becomes the only cookie, hint_s (technical, 7 days, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict), set only after the cookie notice is accepted; the choice stays in local storage. The app uses no cookies.
  • Doctor links: read-only, fixed period, expire in 1–30 days, withdrawable; no email, no account, no cookie.
  • Remote switch-off: the app version travels with every request; settings holds app_min_version, app_blocked, app_off. Owner only.
  • Subscription: Google Play Billing, checked by the server with the Play Developer API; when it runs out, a courteous message to renew.
  • Database: only SHA-256 fingerprints of codes, cookies and links; Anthropic keys encrypted with AES-GCM.
  • Retention: readings older than 365 days deleted every night; acceptances in an append-only register.
  • Personal data: no name, no email (of the Google account only an HMAC-SHA256 fingerprint; the email stays on the phone); photos and files processed and discarded; data in the EU.

Releasing a version

  1. Change on a branch → pull request → merge into main.
  2. GitHub Actions updates D1, deploys the Worker, builds the APK and the AAB, creates release v0.1.N and puts the APK at /download.
  3. The site changes at once; the app updates by installing the new APK.

Extending

The dashboard is modular: lab reports will be a new module in web.ts and app.js, with sign-in, periods, sending and export already in place.
