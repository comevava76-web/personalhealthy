Functional overview

What the app does, who does what and in which order. A blood-pressure diary for Android, with a web dashboard and reports for the doctor.

The flow from the phone to the doctor

The user's path

  1. Installs the app from the public link /download (later from Google Play).
  2. Signs in with Google once, through OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect (a signed ID token, never the password), and gives privacy consent.
  3. Accepts the terms of use, binding, at the first installation and after every update: without acceptance the app does not open. Every acceptance is recorded (pseudonymous account code, phone, versions, text fingerprint, date).
  4. Every day unlocks with the phone's fingerprint or PIN, and stays signed in until “Sign out”.
  5. Imports lab reports by itself: PDF or photo read on the phone, every test (also outside the catalog), one report date; any doubt: nothing saved; never twice.
  6. Records a reading: by voice or with a photo of the monitor (Scan with AI: 15-day trial, then 4.99 $ a year; SYS, DIA, PUL checked and confirmed on screen). The photo is never stored (terms v21).
  7. Looks back: last reading, the week; “Go to your full reports” at the end of Blood pressure or Lab results opens that page of the Web Dashboard in the browser (Blood pressure first: chart, eight tiles, morning/evening balance, pulse; then Lab results).
  8. Shares: the PDF downloaded on the phone, or Web Dashboard → “Send to the doctor”.
  9. Changes phone: signs in with Google and finds everything. Signs out or deletes the account at any time.

Costs and who is paid

The app is free; only the AI features are paid (2.50 $ of AI a year per subscriber).

Fixed rules

  • No diagnosis, no advice, no judgement of the values: no coloured zones, no “normal”.
  • Labels always SYS, DIA, PUL. Pressure and pulse never in the same chart.
  • Charts: 7 days, one dot per day = the mean of that day's readings (explained before the charts), value next to it, axis with day numbers only. Tables show AM/PM.
  • The same A4 PDF from the app and from the web.
  • No red anywhere: SYS violet, DIA teal, PUL amber. No colour means “good” or “bad”.
  • Scan with AI (terms v21): 15-day trial, then 4.99 $ a year (2.50 $ of AI a year); the owner's key only, photo never stored.
  • Owner's powers only from the owner's phone (owner_key; new phone: OWNER_CODE).
  • Readings kept at most 365 days, deleted nightly; acceptances stay as proof.
  • The app stays simple: the owner's controls are only in the web Admin area.

Input: voice and lab reports

How data enters HINT 365: blood pressure by voice or by a photo of the monitor (Scan), lab results from a report. Voice and lab reports are read on the phone; the Scan is read with AI (terms v21): through the Worker to Anthropic with the owner's key.

From the user to the database the input always starts on the user's side

Purple frame: a step done by the user. Voice: the numbers are recognised on the phone and only the three numbers travel. Lab report: the document stays on the phone; only the date and the values as printed travel.

Database tables

persons accounts (pseudonymous account code, Google fingerprint, no email; subscription sub_*) · measurements pressure + lab results · lab_files keyed file fingerprints (no duplicates) · scans, ledger, person_keys left from the removed AI Scan, emptied every night · acceptances accepted terms (versioned evidence) · web_* web access and doctor links · settings, invites · error_log, event_log (import outcomes, codes only) · seen_sigs, rate_limits protection counters; security_snapshot_findings completed scan snapshots.

Security and data

  • Phone: a private key that never leaves the phone signs every request; bad signatures, clocks off by over 5 min and replayed canonical messages are refused; mandatory Google nonce, single-use token and browser revocation on recovery.
  • Limits: hourly/daily caps on sign-ups, sign-ins, links, logs, voice (rate_limits, IP only as SHA-256). CSP and anti-framing headers; robots.txt.
  • Web: a 60 s atomically redeemed code in the # part of the address becomes the only cookie, hint_s (technical, 7 days, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict), set only after consent. The app uses no cookies.
  • Doctor links: read-only, fixed period, expire after 7 days, withdrawable; no sign-in or cookie.
  • One version only: the app version travels with every request; the server accepts only the newest build (APP_VERSION); the app offers Google Play's in-app update. settings (app_min_version, app_blocked, app_off) stay for emergencies (Actions).
  • Owner's area (web, owner only): four numbers only (open vulnerabilities in the app code and in the Android/iOS libraries, open defects, compliance not fully covered), the way to Observability and the version switch-off. Never values or names.
  • Subscription (ready, off until Google Play): Play Billing, checked with the Play Developer API.
  • Retention: readings older than 365 days deleted every night; acceptances in an acceptance register.
  • Personal data: no name, no email (Google account only as HMAC-SHA256); codes, cookies, links only as SHA-256; no third-party API keys; documents read on-device; only lab results and a keyed file fingerprint sync. Source files never uploaded. Cloudflare D1 is EU-bound.

Releasing a version

  1. Change on a branch → pull request → merge into main.
  2. GitHub Actions updates D1, deploys the Worker, builds the APK and the AAB, stores APK/AAB in GitHub release v0.1.N; /download redirects there.
  3. The site changes at once; the app updates by installing the new APK.

Error log continuous testing and improvement

  • Server: failed signed calls (400–599, except 402, 404, 426) and every exception.
  • App: crashes and failed PDF files; at most 20 a session.
  • Web: script errors, at most 5 a page; never on a doctor's link.
  • Short: one row per day, source, code, place, version, with a counter; no free-text messages, so no reading value; 90 days.

Security docs/security/

  • Each PR: 168 security and functional checks; the app must compile.
  • Nightly: live probe; every library (full tree) on OSV.dev; Semgrep; gitleaks → Admin → Security, with Fix (Claude Code).
  • Deadlines: critical 48 h, high 7 days, moderate 30 days. Dependabot PRs on Mondays.
  • Cost: 0 $ extra (GitHub free plan, owner's Claude plan, OSV.dev public).

Who uses it

  • Owner: Admin tab → total errors (90 days); the details reach the developer through the daily issue.
  • Developer: every morning a scheduled routine reads the report of the log and fixes (page 5); in working sessions it is also read after every release.
  • Table: error_log in D1, written by worker/src/errors.ts; app side ErrorReport in Core.kt; web side in app.js.

Data protection GDPR · Swiss FADP

  • Health data, pseudonymous (not anonymous): the GDPR and the Swiss FADP apply. Basis: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)) at sign-in and in the terms (v15, section “Data protection”).
  • Roles: owner = controller; Cloudflare = processor; Google and Anthropic (Scan photo, processor) in the US under their transfer safeguards; the photo goes only to the owner's provider (terms v21).
  • Rights: access, correction, deletion (in the app), a copy, restriction, objection; answer within one month.
  • Gaps still open and the record of processing: docs/compliance/gdpr.md.

Delivery, from the chat to the phone

  1. Decide — Human describes the change in the chat; unclear points are asked, not guessed.
  2. Build and test — Claude on claude/…: code and documents in one commit; harness, pages in Chromium, documents rendered.
  3. Merge — pull request, Docs check and Security tests green, squash merge into main.
  4. Release — local tests → privately signed APK/AAB → Worker + same-revision APK promotion → release record. No missing-key fallback. D1 tables, Worker, APK/AAB v0.1.N, /download; Claude checks it and sends the APK.
  5. Try — Human tries the APK; feedback starts the next round. Rollback: revert the pull request.

Continuous improvement loop proactive: every day, without being asked
