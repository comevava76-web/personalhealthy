Terms of use and notice

Purpose and medical limits

HINT 365 is a personal diary for recording blood pressure and confirmed lab results and displaying their history. It does not measure, diagnose, judge whether values are normal or recommend treatment. Your doctor assesses the results. Never change treatment because of the app. For an emergency contact a doctor or emergency services (144 in Switzerland, 112 in Europe).

Personal use and accuracy

One account is one person. Record readings and upload lab reports only of yourself, the holder of this account: never of another person. HINT 365 does not know whose data they are and cannot tell them apart, so data of someone else would mix with your history. Reading by voice, from a photo of the monitor and from documents can be wrong or incomplete. Check blood-pressure values before saving, and lab results against the original report before relying on them or sharing them. Protect your phone with a screen lock. Sharing a PDF or link is a deliberate action by you; a recipient can keep or forward it.

Account, storage and providers

Sign in with Google uses OAuth 2.0/OpenID Connect. HINT 365 does not receive your Google password. The server stores an HMAC of your Google account identifier, not your email. Confirmed pressure readings are stored with your account in Cloudflare D1 in the EU for up to 365 days and purged daily; lab results stay until you delete them (never automatically). Both they are pseudonymized, not anonymous. Google recovery reconnects your synchronized history. Blood pressure is recorded by voice or with a photo of the monitor. The phone’s speech service may use its own network processing. The photo Scan reads the monitor display with artificial intelligence. The Scan is an AI feature: it is on at once, free for 15 days from the first Scan, then with the AI features subscription; in every case at most 3 Scans a day. The trial is given once per Google account and per phone: for this the server keeps an HMAC fingerprint of the Google account and of the phone’s identifier with the trial start date, also after the account is deleted, for at most 2 years and only for this purpose. The photo of the display goes through HINT 365's server to Anthropic (Claude), which reads the three numbers for HINT 365 and does not use them to train its models; the server passes the photo on without storing or logging it, and the photo carries no name or account. In every case the numbers read are shown to you and saved only if you confirm them, and the photo is then deleted from the phone. Only the three numbers you confirmed are stored by HINT 365. Acceptance records are retained as evidence after account deletion; technical error codes are retained for 90 days without free-text messages. Details and rights are described in the privacy policy. The web uses only the technical session cookie hint_s (seven days) and the stored cookie choice; Android uses no cookies.

To switch it on again you need the AI features subscription, which covers its costs: 4.99 USD per year or the amount displayed by Google Play, paid to the app owner, valid for one year. This number is only an estimate, not a guaranteed value: the number of Scans the allowance permits depends solely on the prices of the artificial intelligence provider that reads the photos (today Anthropic), which the owner does not control and which can change at any time; the Scans possible may therefore be more or fewer. The owner accepts no liability if the Scans actually possible are fewer than estimated: the subscription gives the right to the 2.50 USD allowance, not to a number of Scans. Lab report import and history

Where the data are. At rest, the document stays only on your phone, wherever you keep it: PDFs and photos selected in Lab results are read and processed exclusively on the phone, and temporary copies are deleted after processing. In transit, the document never leaves the phone: only the report date and the results as printed (test, value, unit, reference interval) and a keyed fingerprint of the file travel, over HTTPS, an encrypted connection (TLS; Cloudflare uses TLS 1.3 when the phone supports it), each request also signed with a key kept on the phone, without name, surname, date of birth or any other reference to the person. On the server, only these are stored, linked to your pseudonymous account. They are pseudonymized, not anonymous: they carry no name, but they are linked to your account and remain health data under the GDPR and the Swiss FADP. The document on your phone, and the phone itself, remain your responsibility (screen lock, backups, apps you share it with). The results are saved automatically only when the whole document was read with certainty: one report date and every result line understood; otherwise nothing is saved. The same file, or the same test on the same date, is never saved twice. Only the test code or the test name as printed, value, unit, reference interval, report date and a keyed fingerprint of the file (used only to recognize the same file again) are stored with your account, also for an older report, until you delete them: a date on the web, a report in the app, all lab results on the web, or your whole account. They are never deleted automatically, so your history stays complete; they are available on the web and on a replacement phone. A result outside the reference printed on the same report is shown in orange with an arrow: this compares numbers only and is not a medical assessment. You are responsible for selecting only your own reports, protecting your phone and checking the results against the original. Extraction may be incomplete or incorrect and does not provide medical interpretation. This responsibility does not exclude the provider’s applicable obligations. Results are compared only within the same test and unit; no diagnosis or treatment recommendation is provided.
