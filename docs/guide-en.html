HINT

HealthyInstantTracker

User guide

The blood-pressure diary: record readings by voice or with a photo, and prepare the report for the doctor.

1. What HINT is

HINT is a diary: it records the values of your blood-pressure monitor, SYS (systolic), DIA (diastolic) and PUL (pulse), with date and time, and shows them in tables, charts and reports.

HINT is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. It does not assess the values, does not say whether they are normal or high, makes no diagnosis and does not replace the doctor. The assessment is always the doctor's.

3. Installing

  1. On the Android phone open https://personalhealthy-api.comevava76.workers.dev/download and download HINT.apk.
  2. Open the file: Android asks to allow installs from this source. Allow it.
  3. Open HINT, read the note about your data, tick "I have read and accept" and tap "Sign in with Google".
  4. From then on the app opens with the phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock.

Google is used only to create your diary and to find it again on a new phone. It is not asked day to day.

4. Recording a reading

🎤 Record (free)

  1. Tap Record and say the three values in order: SYS, DIA, PUL (e.g. "127, 80, 70"). Pauses are fine.
  2. The app stops by itself once it has heard three numbers and shows them.
  3. Check them and tap Save. Date and time are those of the moment you spoke.

The app refuses impossible values (for example DIA higher than SYS, a missing number) and marks unusual ones in amber, to check again.

📷 Scan (optional, paid)

  1. Tap Scan and photograph the monitor's display: close, straight, without reflections.
  2. The app reads the numbers and shows them. Check they match the display and tap Save.

5. Turning on scanning (AI credit)

At first start the app shows a welcome screen explaining the two ways: you can tap "Continue without scanning" and turn it on later from Admin → Token → "Add my key". There are four steps, each with its button:

  1. Create an Anthropic account (free, with your email).
  2. Add credit: load a small prepaid credit (a few dollars last for months).
  3. Create a key: it starts with sk-ant-; copy it.
  4. Save the key here: back in the app, tap "Paste the key", type the credit you see on Anthropic and tap Save.

The key is tested and stored encrypted on the server; it is never shown again, not even to you. You can replace or delete it at any time. The balance in the app is an estimate: the real one is on Anthropic.

7. Reminders

Every day at 9:00 and 17:00 "Remember to measure your blood pressure" arrives, with a number on the icon. At midnight they are cleared. Turn them off in the phone settings ("Measurement reminders").

8. New or lost phone

Install HINT on the new phone and tap "Sign in with Google" with the same account: all your readings are there. The old phone is disconnected. To update the app, install the new version over the existing one, without uninstalling.

9. Your data

  • What is saved: the email and identifier of your Google account (only to find your diary again), your readings with date and time and their source (photo or voice), and your encrypted Anthropic key if you add it.
  • Photos and voice: the photo is sent to Anthropic only to read the numbers and is not kept by the app. Speech is transcribed by the phone; only the three numbers reach the app.
  • Where: on Cloudflare servers, with the database in the European Union. The app's code is on GitHub.
  • Who sees it: only you. Other users do not. The person who runs the service can reach the database only for maintenance.
  • Deleting: in Admin you can delete one reading, all readings, or the account with all its data. It is immediate and final.

Always check the values. Photo reading and speech recognition can be wrong: before saving, compare the numbers with the monitor's display.
