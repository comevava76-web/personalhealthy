User guide

The blood-pressure diary: record readings by voice, import lab reports, and prepare the report for the doctor.

1. What HINT 365 is

HINT 365 is a diary: it records the values of your blood-pressure monitor, SYS (systolic), DIA (diastolic) and PUL (pulse), with date and time, and shows them in tables, charts and reports.

HINT 365 is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. It does not assess the values, never says whether they are normal or high, makes no diagnosis and does not replace your doctor. Assessment is always up to the doctor.

3. Installing and updating

  1. On your Android phone open https://personalhealthy-api.comevava76.workers.dev/download and download HINT.apk.
  2. Open the file: Android asks to allow installing from this source. Allow it.
  3. Tap "Sign in with Google": sign-in uses the OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect standards, and HINT 365 never receives your password.
  4. Read the terms of use and accept them: they appear at the first installation and after every update. Without acceptance the app does not open.
  5. From then on the app opens with the phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock. If the phone has no screen lock at all, the app opens unprotected and Blood pressure shows an orange notice that leads to the settings: protecting the phone is your responsibility.

To update, install the new version over the existing one, without uninstalling. If an old version is switched off, the app shows only the button to download the latest: your data stay.

4. Recording a reading

🎤 Record

  1. Tap Record and say the three values in order: SYS, DIA, PUL (e.g. "127, 80, 70"). You can pause.
  2. The app stops by itself once it has heard three numbers and shows you the values.
  3. Check them and tap Save reading. Date and time are those of the moment you spoke.

The app refuses impossible values (for example DIA higher than SYS, a missing number) and marks unusual ones in orange, to check again.

5. No artificial intelligence

HINT 365 no longer uses artificial intelligence and sends no data to any AI provider. The monitor Scan with Anthropic has been removed (terms of use version 19): readings are recorded by voice, free. If you had saved an Anthropic key, the server deletes it together with the costs and readings of that feature; if you wish, delete it from your Anthropic account too.

7. Web Dashboard and the doctor

  1. Tap Reports in the bar at the bottom (or Go to your full reports at the end of Blood pressure and Lab results): the browser opens already signed in to your account (the link lasts 60 seconds).
  2. The first time, accept the technical cookie that keeps you signed in for 7 days. It is the only cookie: no advertising, statistics or third parties. The Cookies link at the bottom shows your choice and changes it; there is no Sign out button: the session ends by itself.
  3. You see large charts of the last 7 days and the list of readings, and you can download the same A4 PDF as the app.
  4. Send to the doctor: choose Email or WhatsApp. The PDF of the report and a read-only link valid 7 days go together, with a ready message in the phone's language ("Good morning, I am sending the blood pressure report recorded in the period …"; email subject "Blood pressure report"). On a phone the PDF is attached for you; on a computer it is downloaded and you attach it. You can withdraw the links at any time.

    5. 8. Reminders

    Every day at 9:00 and 17:00 "Remember to measure your blood pressure" arrives, with a number on the icon. They reset at midnight. Turn them off in the phone's settings ("Measurement reminders").

    9. New or lost phone

    Install HINT 365 on the new phone and tap "Sign in with Google" with the same account: you find all your readings. The old phone is signed out.

    10. Your data

    • What is saved: your readings with date, time and source (voice, or photo for readings made with the former Scan); of your Google account only an encrypted fingerprint, never the email, which stays on your phone.
    • Voice: speech is transcribed by the phone; only the three numbers reach the server.
    • Where and for how long: on Cloudflare, database in the European Union. Readings are kept for at most 365 days: older ones are deleted every night. Export PDF or Excel to keep them longer.
    • Who sees them: only you, and the doctor with the link you send. The owner of the app sees only usage numbers tied to an anonymous code (when you joined, last opened, how many readings), never values or reports. Technical errors go into a short error log, without reading values, kept 90 days, to fix problems.
    • Deletion: in Admin you can delete one reading, all readings or the account. It is immediate and final; only the proof of the accepted terms remains.

    Lab results

    Only your own reports. One account is one person: upload only your own reports and readings, never those of a relative. HINT 365 does not know whose data they are and cannot separate them (terms of use).

    1. Open Lab results → Import lab report and choose the laboratory’s PDF or a photo of the report (20 MB maximum; unencrypted PDFs up to 15 pages). Other kinds of file cannot be chosen.
    2. The app does the rest, also if you switch tab: a bar fills through Processing → Scanning → Uploading; the outcome stays a few seconds, then the bar goes away and the outcome is in the upload history. The document is read only on the phone; the original, patient identifiers and full text are not uploaded.
    3. It is saved only when everything is clear: one report date and every result line understood, also tests outside our catalog (with the name as printed). If anything is unclear nothing is saved and the app says why.
    4. Never twice: the same file, or the same test on the same date, is not saved again. An older report is saved with its own date. Results are never deleted by themselves: they stay until you delete them. The document stays on the phone; only the date and the values as printed reach the server, encrypted in transit, never a name or any reference to the person.
    5. On the web, Lab results shows a table: one row per test, one column per report date; a dash where a test is missing. In orange with ↑ or ↓: a value outside the reference printed on the report (a comparison only, not an assessment). Delete under a date removes that column; the report can then be imported again. Delete all lab results, under the table, removes them all. Share and PDF give the same table.

    The app and web follow the device/browser language: Italian, English, German or French (English fallback). Medical interpretation belongs to your doctor.
